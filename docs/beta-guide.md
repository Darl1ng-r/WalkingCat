# StrayCat Walk — Beta Tester Guide

Welcome to the **StrayCat Walk** private beta! Thank you for helping us test the app and explore your neighborhood.

---

## 1. Beta Objectives
Our core question for this 2-week beta is:
> **Does StrayCat Walk make walking more enjoyable, motivating, and mindful?**

We are testing with 30–50 walkers on iOS (via Apple TestFlight) and Android (via Google Play Internal App Sharing).

---

## 2. Getting Started

1. **Install the App**:
   - **iOS**: Accept the TestFlight invite received by email and install build `1.0.0 (1)`.
   - **Android**: Click the Google Play Internal Testing link and tap "Download on Google Play".
2. **First Run & Permissions**:
   - Tap **Get Started** and create your walker account.
   - Grant **Location Permission** ("While Using App" and "Always Allow" for background recording).
   - Connect **Apple Health** (iOS) or **Health Connect** (Android) to sync your daily steps.
   - Review our **Cat Welfare Promise** (always keep ≥ 3 meters distance; flash is permanently disabled).

---

## 3. What to Test (Key Scenarios)

### Scenario A: Your First Outdoor Walk
- Go to the **Walk** tab and tap **Start Walk**.
- Walk outdoors for at least 5–10 minutes.
- Check lock-screen / background tracking: lock your phone or switch to another app for a couple minutes.
- Finish your walk, check the summary stats, and notice the **Route Privacy Trimming** (the first and last 150 meters are automatically hidden from the map).

### Scenario B: Respectful Stray Cat Spotting
- While walking, if you see a community or stray cat, open the **Cat Camera**.
- Notice the **Tips banner** and test the optical/digital zoom buttons (`1x`, `1.5x`, `2x`, `3x`).
- Take a photo from ≥ 3 meters.
- Watch the AI cat-card generation in real time (takes 2–5 seconds).
- Once accepted, view your new **Collectible Cat Card** in the **Collection** tab!

### Scenario C: Friendly Competition & Community
- Go to the **Social** tab.
- Tap **Share Invite Link** and send an invite to a friend or fellow beta tester.
- Once connected, check the **Weekly Friends Leaderboard** (Steps, Distance, Walks, Challenge Pts). Notice calories are excluded to maintain a friendly, non-punitive experience.
- Share one of your cat cards with friends and react with 👏, 🐾, ❤️, or 🔥.

### Scenario D: Privacy & Data Controls
- Go to **Profile > Privacy**:
  - Test changing your route privacy trim buffer (100 m, 150 m, 300 m).
  - Test toggling leaderboard visibility.
  - Test toggling anonymous analytics telemetry.
- Go to **Profile > Account & Data**:
  - Test **Export My Data** to download your JSON archive.

## 4. How to Report Bugs & Send Feedback

You can send feedback directly inside the app:
1. Open the **Profile** tab.
2. Scroll to Settings and tap **💌 Send Beta Feedback**.
3. Choose a category:
   - 🐛 **Bug Report**: Something crashed, froze, or GPS lost tracking.
   - 💡 **Feature Idea**: Something you wished existed during your walk.
   - 🐾 **Praise & Cats**: Stories from your walk or cute cat discoveries.
   - 💬 **General Feedback**: Anything else.
4. Type your thoughts and tap **Submit Beta Feedback**.

You can also reach the team via email at: `beta@walkingcat.app`.

---

## 5. Known Limitations & Notes
- GPS battery consumption: Expected battery drain during an active 60-minute walk with screen off is ~8–10%. If you experience higher drain, please report your device model.
- Cat validation uses cloud AI; if you are in an area without mobile data, the sighting photo is safely buffered on your device and uploads automatically when you reconnect.
- Flash is permanently disabled by software design for animal welfare reasons.

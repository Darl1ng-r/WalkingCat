# 99 — Post-MVP Backlog (Do NOT build during the MVP)

> These ideas come from the product overview. They are parked until the beta proves people walk more. Each needs its own phase doc (same template) before implementation, informed by `docs/beta-results.md`.

## A. Collection & discovery
- **Assisted same-cat matching:** suggest "This might be Whiskers" using image embeddings — suggestion only, user confirms; show uncertainty.
- **Card themes & cosmetics** unlocked with points; profile decorations.
- **Gallery uploads** with stronger anti-abuse (recency checks, manual review).
- **Seasonal collections / events.**

## B. Community cat map (high risk — requires safeguards first)
- Approximate-area heatmap (coarse geohash cells, minimum k-anonymity threshold, time delay).
- Community sighting records with uncertainty, reporting of sensitive/outdated info.
- Pre-requisites: moderation team, abuse analysis, animal-welfare review, delayed & fuzzed locations, opt-in contributions.

## C. Animal-welfare extension (requires real partners)
- Directory of local animal-welfare organizations.
- Voluntary "cat may need help" report routed to a verified partner.
- Transparent partner-managed campaigns/donations.
- Never: rescue claims, medical advice, promises of assistance.

## D. Social
- Private groups & group leaderboards.
- Shared/group challenges with friends.
- Richer feed, comments (with moderation).
- Universal links for invites.

## E. Activity
- Per-user local-timezone challenge windows.
- Workout write-back to Apple Health / Health Connect (opt-in).
- Apple Watch / Wear OS companion.
- Live Activity / ongoing notification with walk stats.

## F. Monetization (only after retention is validated)
- Freemium subscription (advanced insights, extra customization).
- Cosmetic purchases.
- Clearly labelled sponsored challenges.
- **Never** sell location/activity data or target users by cat-sighting locations.

## G. Platform
- Web companion (collection viewer) via Expo web + EAS Hosting.
- Admin/moderation web dashboard.
- Arabic/RTL and other localizations.
